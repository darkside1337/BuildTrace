A workspace built around the life of a component.

A Mobbin reference study and full redesign plan for receiving PC parts, managing stock, assembling builds, and finding recorded warranty history.

Evidence boundary. Mobbin MCP required a paid plan. Browser research succeeded using normally accessible screen highlights and related-screen links. Five apps were inspected; five readable screen exports are embedded below. Full receiving, reservation, counting, and warranty flows remain unavailable. This is a bounded screen study, not an exhaustive competitor audit or a tested redesign.

The recommendation

Redesign BuildTrace as a component ledger: a calm, connected operational workspace where every quantity, serial, receipt, and build leads to its underlying record.

The strongest references support a stable shell, clearly grouped forms, contextual secondary information, and focused actions. BuildTrace’s distinctive opportunity is the connection between records. Its interface should make “where did this unit come from, where is it now, and who received it?” visible without a scavenger hunt.

Keep the emerald identity and legible working surfaces. Replace the page composition, record hierarchy, and workflow interaction patterns across the product. A full redesign does not require discarding a useful brand color or adding features outside the PRD.

What the comparable apps do well

Each reference below pairs a visible pattern with a proposed adaptation. Static screenshots establish layout and labels; they do not prove keyboard behavior, persistence, error handling, accessibility, or task speed.

Primary reference · product administration

Shopify

Observed: a persistent sidebar and top workspace bar frame the Add product screen. Main content groups title, description, media, category, and price. A narrower column holds status, publishing, and product organization. Save/discard sit in the top bar; a setup guide occupies a separate right panel.

Design strength: core content, secondary configuration, and onboarding assistance have distinct places. The white panels and restrained gray shell make hierarchy readable without a different accent for every field.

Apply: put part identity and tracking mode first; place warranty defaults and threshold settings in secondary groups. Keep catalog creation separate from stock entry. Use a clear unsaved state and explicit save action.

Adapt carefully: remove storefront publishing, tax, media management, and the AI guide from our composition. Those serve Shopify’s scope, not this MVP.

Primary reference · item entry

Square

Observed: Create item uses a broad column for names, prices, description, and image, with status, categories, menus, and channel information in a secondary column. Save is isolated at the top right. The required name label is explicit.

Design strength: a focused editor emphasizes the information needed to define an item, while keeping secondary setup visible. Dividers and small section labels provide structure.

Apply: give receiving a dedicated working page: editable receipt lines in the main area, receipt identity and review summary beside them, and a single prominent Post receipt action. Use shorter forms for catalog setup.

Adapt carefully: a receipt with many lines and serials needs a page, not a constrained modal. Receiving review is our proposal; this screenshot does not demonstrate Square’s receiving behavior.

Supporting reference · structured records

Zoho CRM

Observed: Create Product separates Product Information, Price Information, and Stock Information. Fields align in two columns. The sidebar exposes an Inventory group. Save, Save and New, and Cancel are consistently located in the page toolbar.

Design strength: section boundaries make a long record form easier to scan. Repeated field alignment helps users locate values across records.

Apply: standardize section order across part, supplier, customer, and coverage forms. Group identity, operational rules, recorded coverage, and notes. Required information comes before optional fields.

Adapt carefully: Zoho’s dense navigation and many enterprise fields would overwhelm a small PC shop. Do not copy its commission, tax, or sales lifecycle fields; keep currency values and warranty dates semantically separate.

Supporting reference · contextual creation

HubSpot

Observed: a Create Product panel opens on the right while the underlying Products empty state remains visible. The panel has a named header, close affordance, vertically stacked fields, and Create Product/Cancel actions at its bottom.

Design strength: creation preserves the context that prompted it. The empty state explains the task and offers creation/import choices.

Apply: use a Base UI sheet for a short “New customer” or “New supplier” form launched inside build or receipt setup, then return the created record to that workflow. On a phone, use a full-width presentation.

Adapt carefully: preserve drafts, focus return, errors, and deliberate dismissal. Do not put lengthy serial entry or CSV review in a narrow sheet. Billing frequency and margin calculation are outside BuildTrace’s scope.

Limited reference · shell & first use

Linear

Observed: the welcome screen keeps workspace and team navigation on the left, leaves a quiet content area, and offers contextual starter tasks. A second accessible highlight shows an AI conversation rather than a status board or issue list.

Design strength: restrained chrome makes the current task the center of attention. Navigation groups communicate context without enclosing every region in a heavy box.

Apply: carry that restraint into BuildTrace’s shell and offer a concrete first task in genuinely empty workspaces. Make our entry “Receive a shipment” or “Add a part,” not an open-ended prompt.

Evidence limit: do not claim that a build board, allocation workflow, or status model was verified from these screens. AI-first onboarding is not a fit for BuildTrace’s MVP.

The proposed visual direction

A precise ledger on a shop workbench: neutral structure, emerald intent, and a visible trail from receipt to delivered PC.

Visual system

  • Surfaces: white working area, a quiet neutral shell, thin separators; no repeated floating metric cards.
  • Accent: retain Stockroom Emerald for primary actions, current navigation, and focus. Keep warning/error semantics independent.
  • Typography: 28–30px page titles, 18–20px section headings, 14–16px working text, 12–13px supporting metadata. Phone inputs use 16px. Use tabular numbers for quantities and USD.
  • Geometry: 2px buttons, 4px inputs, 8px dialogs; sharper table and section edges. Product identity, rather than decoration, creates character.
  • Density: 48–56px interactive rows and 44px minimum targets. Compact metadata stays subordinate to model names and available quantity.

Layout system

  • Persistent 224px sidebar at desktop widths; top workspace bar with shop context and cross-record lookup entry.
  • 40px desktop gutters, 24px tablet gutters, 16px phone gutters. Wider working pages for receipt/build tables; forms use a readable, bounded column.
  • A main record column plus a 280–320px secondary summary where it helps the task. Stack summary before consequential actions on mobile.
  • Use continuous tables for comparison, sheets for brief contextual creation, and full pages for multi-line work. Do not put a wizard around every form.
  • Motion only explains state changes, at roughly 120–180ms; reduced motion removes it. Critical feedback remains visible in the record.

Phone & tablet

At 360–430px, product rows become identity-first summaries with three labeled quantities. Receiving shows one line and its serials at a time, with an overview of completion and blockers. Keep consequential actions reachable without covering the last field; account for safe areas and the onscreen keyboard.

At tablet widths, prioritize the working table over permanent navigation. Use a drawer for navigation and stack secondary summaries. Horizontal scrolling is limited to a named comparison region, never the entire page.

Keyboard & accessibility

Use real links and native table semantics; Base UI dialogs/sheets manage focus, Escape and return focus. Every action works without hover. Status includes text; error summaries link to controls. Meet WCAG AA contrast and inspect at 200% zoom and enlarged text.

Keyboard scanner input must preserve exact serial characters. Restore focus after contextual record creation. Announce result counts and confirmed mutations without forcing screen readers through a full-page rerender.

How to deliver the redesign

Build vertical slices against the existing roadmap. Shared visual foundations come first, but a screen is complete only when its supported flow, states, server boundaries, and verification work together.

Research closure

Fill the evidence gaps

With Pro access, inspect actual inventory filtering, receiving review, serial capture, stock reconciliation, linked-record detail, and build/status flows. Gate: a reference-to-workflow matrix distinguishes observed behavior, static appearance, and our own decisions.

Foundation + catalog
Roadmap 00–01

Establish the record grammar

Resolve the visual direction, update DESIGN.md, and reconcile the typography split. Implement shell/header/list toolbar/record sections/status vocabulary in components/ and tokens in app/globals.css. Apply first to the existing catalog list, create/edit, detail and archive flow.

Gate: authenticated walkthrough, both OAuth providers, duplicate SKU/error recovery, cross-shop checks, phone layout and zoom. Preserve existing acceptance gaps rather than marking Phase 01 complete on visual work alone.

Access + stock + intake
Roadmap 02–08

Make receiving the first complete operational slice

Complete Owner-managed shop settings and Staff access in Phase 02. Extend catalog detail to genuine units/lots and balances, then supplier → draft → manual posting → partial remainder → correction → CSV draft review. Introduce the contextual sheet, line editor, serial group, review summary and immutable receipt detail alongside their actual data contracts.

Gate: 10 prepared serialized units within the PRD’s proposed 3-minute target; invalid line prevents all posting; retry adds no duplicate movements. Record task conditions, not a claimed customer improvement.

Build + delivery
Roadmap 09–12

Make allocations visible

Customer creation → draft build → reserve → substitute/release → assembly → ready → delivery. Build the allocation table and blocker summary; deliver only from complete allocations. Keep the same record grammar used in receiving.

Gate: two builds competing for one unit; failed replacement preserves original reservation; delivery deducts once and preserves traceability.

Count + trace
Roadmap 13–14

Close the component’s history

Phase 13: lookup → unit/build trail → separate coverage records. Phase 14: count/reconciliation → reasoned adjustment. Use a shared linked-record identity treatment across suppliers, receipts, builds, customers and units.

Gate: known serial/receipt lookup within the proposed 30-second target; unknown coverage remains unknown; counts reconcile with movements and reservations.

Overview + demo
Roadmap 15–18

Make the portfolio story assessable

Ship useful overview links, then a populated isolated demo, safe reset/expiry, and publication verification. Replace the ordinary public connection-check entry with the completed product journey once it exists.

Gate: a visitor completes the central demo workflow within the proposed 10-minute target; one visitor cannot access or reset another shop.

Reusable implementation contracts

  • Shared components: PageHeader, ListToolbar, StockSummary, RecordIdentity, StatusBadge, LinkedRecordTrail, MutationSummary, ReceiptLineEditor and SerialCaptureGroup. Keep feature-specific orchestration with its owning feature.
  • Actions and reads: keep shop checks on the server; use serializable ActionResult and inline errors; preserve Next.js redirect/not-found control flow. No stock changes through presentation state.
  • Transactions: the inventory domain owns receiving, reservations, corrections and delivery stock writes. Visual redesign must preserve atomicity, idempotency and historical snapshots.
  • Documentation: DESIGN.md owns approved visuals; PRD owns scope; ARCHITECTURE owns boundaries; ROADMAP owns completion. This report proposes work and does not complete any roadmap item.
  • Verification: focused domain/integration checks for mutations, Playwright for complete journeys, desktop/tablet/phone visual inspection, keyboard/scanner walkthrough, long IDs and 200% zoom. Read the installed Next.js guides before subsequent app implementation.

First implementation slice: the existing catalog flow and shared shell, followed by receiving. Do not spend the redesign budget styling placeholder pages. The complete navigation above is a target IA, not permission to expose inactive destinations.

Official documentation used as supplementary context

Shopify’s inventory adjustment documentation describes pending review before saving, inventory-state columns and adjustment history. This supports the review-before-commit concept, but BuildTrace requires stronger mandatory reasons and domain-specific receiving rules.

RepairShopr’s official feature list includes serial-to-invoice warranty tracking, making it a close domain reference; its repair, POS and invoicing scope exceeds this MVP. Sortly’s official site positions it around inventory, supplies, materials and tools. Both belong on a future domain-research shortlist; no visual-quality assessment is claimed for them here.

Linear’s board documentation describes board/list grouping and constraints. A build board is an optional hypothesis to test, not a recommended default established by the observed highlights.
